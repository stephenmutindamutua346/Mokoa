Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is the delivery in Kutus? ▼
We guarantee delivery within 60 minutes for standard items like gas and water within Kutus town and a 5km radius.

Yes, all our plumbers, electricians, and movers undergo strict background checks and skill assessments before joining our platform.

We currently accept M-Pesa (Paybill and Till Number) and Cash on Delivery for select services.
